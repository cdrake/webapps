Add dcm2niix, a browser-only catalog app that converts one MRI examination at a time into a reviewed BIDS dataset. Accept files, folders and ZIP archives; review anatomical, diffusion and reverse phase encoding acquisitions; enter missing metadata; and download an organized BIDS dataset with an explicit identifying-metadata policy. Preserve separate acquisitions and image/gradient ordering, and link reverse EPI fieldmaps to their DWI targets. Include a pinned DICOM example, Philips native-reference checks and offline/interface workflow coverage.
