The catalog cards now show what each app reads (anatomy, modality and input formats), whether it needs WebGPU, and whether it has a command line or a compute-server option, in place of the first three keywords. Descriptions are rewritten as one plain sentence each, task first, at most 100 characters; they also appear in each app's bar and Cite text. The categories are reorganized into ten groups of at least two apps: Lesion Mapping, Reconstruction & Synthesis and Surfaces & Meshes are new, Normalization joins Registration, and spectroscopy joins Quantitative MRI. `docs/architecture/catalog-metadata-audit.md` records the audit and its open findings.
