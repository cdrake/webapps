The catalog cards now show what each app reads (anatomy, modality and input formats), whether it needs WebGPU, and whether it has a command line or a compute-server option, in place of the first three keywords. Descriptions that named a method or output instead of the task are rewritten, task first, within the 60-character app bar limit. The categories are reorganized into ten groups of at least two apps: Lesion Mapping, Reconstruction & Synthesis and Surfaces & Meshes are new, Normalization joins Registration, and spectroscopy joins Quantitative MRI. `docs/architecture/catalog-metadata-audit.md` records the audit and its open findings.
